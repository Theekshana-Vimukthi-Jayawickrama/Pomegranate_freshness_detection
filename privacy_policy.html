PomeScan Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how PomeScan ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Images Captured by Camera

Our app requires camera permission to capture images of pomegranates for freshness detection analysis. These images are:

  • Processed locally on your device using AI models
  • Used only for the app's core functionality (freshness detection)
  • Not stored permanently on our servers
  • Not shared with third parties

Chat Feature Data

When you use the chat feature to ask questions about pomegranate freshness:

  • Your questions and chat history are sent to Google Gemini AI service
  • AI model results from your image analysis are included in the conversation
  • This data is transmitted over the internet to provide AI-powered responses
  • Data is processed according to Google's Privacy Policy

Technical Data

We may collect anonymous technical information such as:

  • App usage statistics
  • Device information (operating system, app version)
  • Error logs to improve app performance

This data does not identify individual users.

How We Use Your Information

  • Core Functionality: Images are analyzed locally to determine pomegranate freshness
  • AI Assistance: Text queries and results are sent to Google Gemini for generating helpful responses about pomegranates
  • App Improvement: Anonymous usage data helps us improve the app

Data Storage and Security

  • Images are processed in real-time and not stored permanently
  • Chat conversations are temporary and stored only during your session
  • All local processing happens on your device
  • We implement appropriate security measures to protect any data in transit

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties, except:

  • Google Gemini AI: Text data (questions and AI results) is sent to Google for processing chat responses
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law

Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
